## Changes

- Open match archive details in a dedicated workspace page with explicit
  View details actions, clickable rows, and direct links to individual matches.
- Navigate to the previous or next match in the filtered and sorted archive,
  including across pages. Restore filters, sorting, pagination, scroll position,
  and keyboard focus when returning to the archive.
- Redesign scoreboards with Core, Combat, and Support tabs, a personal performance
  summary, and expandable player metrics, awards, and profile access.
- Show teams side by side when space permits and stack them in narrower windows,
  with contained table scrolling and accessible navigation controls.
- Bring replay selection, BATrace access, match ID copying, and refresh into the
  detail view. Preserve displayed data and the selected metric tab during refresh.
