## Changes

- Save new recordings as MP4 with automatic AV1, HEVC, and H.264 encoder
  selection and a software AV1 fallback. Replace fixed bitrate with a quality
  score, defaulting to 20, while retaining playback of existing WebM recordings.
- Improve workspace and Settings navigation with breadcrumbs, clearer active
  states, and a shared resizable sidebar that remembers its width.
- Refine match archives, scoreboards, player reports, and Settings layouts for
  smaller windows, long names, and both interface languages. Preserve search,
  scroll position, and keyboard focus when returning from details.
- Unify glass surfaces and controls across light and dark themes, improve switch
  contrast and submenu access, and prevent palette hints from repainting nearby
  previews. Make previous-room summaries and profile name history easier to read.
