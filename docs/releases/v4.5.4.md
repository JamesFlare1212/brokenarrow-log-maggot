## Changes

- Open the match archive in grid view by default and enter match details by
  clicking a card or its map heading. Keep player profile actions independent
  and restore the selected view and keyboard focus when returning.
- Show colored title and score badges beside player names in match scoreboards,
  with the same explanations and pinned popovers as Radar.
- Give player names and awards more room by sizing numeric scoreboard columns
  to their contents and allowing badges to wrap within the player column.
- Improve match detail scrolling with a scrollbar at the window edge and a
  toolbar that stays directly below the titlebar while scrolling.
