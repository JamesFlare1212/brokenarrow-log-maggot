## Changes

- Keep the overview's latest ELO and recent win rate available after leaving a
  room or closing the game by restoring the last recognized account. Switch to
  newly recognized accounts without mixing their match histories.
- Replace the overview's room-player count with the recording library count and
  a video icon. Refresh the count when recordings change or the save folder is
  switched, and provide retry feedback when the folder cannot be read.
- Highlight available updates in the sidebar with a notification indicator and
  a clearer download action, including when the sidebar is collapsed.
