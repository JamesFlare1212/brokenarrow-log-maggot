## Changes

- Open older matches without an online match ID as local log details. Show saved
  players, duration, total points, and decks with clear missing-data labels, and
  preserve navigation and keyboard focus. Keep unavailable online actions disabled.
- Recognize PvE scenarios from game logs and reprocess existing archives to update
  their modes. Known PvE details and refreshes use local records instead of
  requesting unavailable BATrace match data.
- Add direct per-match refresh and a compact archive action menu. Improve archive
  column sizing, including English labels for unrecorded data, and retain local
  details when navigating between old and online matches.
